# B3: potential-based progress shaping

*Step 4, trained 2026-11-08. Seeds: 7, 8, 9. [All attempts](README.md) · [Experiment notes](../EXPERIMENT_NOTES.md)*

**Status:** FRONT still 0; BACK/LEFT/RIGHT equal to the scripted driver's successes, with many more crashes.

## Idea

Per-decision credit for closing the distance to the slot, as a potential: 0.1 × (0.99 Φ′ − Φ), Φ = −distance. It
telescopes, so it changes how fast credit arrives but cannot change which policy is best. It was the cheapest test of
whether FRONT lacked only a learning signal.

*Prediction:* FRONT reached in at least 5 of 62 episodes on two seeds.

## Approach

B2 plus the progress term, starting from B0's trained policy; full task, 1M transitions.

| Run | Arm | Task | Reward | Transitions | Starts from | Trained on |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `B0` | full task | +50 reach, crash 0 | 1M | fresh weights | PC |
| 2 | `B3` | full task | +50 reach, −50 crash, progress 0.1/m | 1M | `B0`'s trained policy (same seed) | PC |

## Reward function

Of the last run, `B3` (per decision, in goal units after the reward scale):

- +50 when ego reaches the slot (the episode ends)
- −50 on a crash (the episode ends)
- +0.1 × (γ·Φ(next) − Φ(now)) per decision, Φ = −(distance to the slot in m), γ = 0.99: potential-based progress, which cannot change the best policy
- 0 on a timeout (25 s) or a lost anchor

Written out: `r = 50·[reached] − 50·[crash] + 0.1·(0.99·Φ′ − Φ)`, where [reached] and [crash] are 1 on the decision the episode ends that way and 0 otherwise.

Earlier runs of the pipeline used:

- `B0`: +50 reach, crash 0 (`r = 50·[reached]`)
