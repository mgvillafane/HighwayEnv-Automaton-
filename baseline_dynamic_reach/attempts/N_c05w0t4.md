# N_c05w0t4: no practice, c05w0t4

*Step 8c2, trained 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-20. Seeds: 7, 10, 13. [All attempts](README.md) · [Experiment notes](../EXPERIMENT_NOTES.md)*

**Status:** Screened in Step 8c2 on 3 seeds; not a finalist.

## Idea

**Order N, no practice.** All three rounds are on the full task. Round 1 is success only (B0's lesson: learn to reach before any crash cost); rounds 2 and 3 use the reward under test. It asks whether practice is needed at all.

**Crash cost 0.5 × goal.** Discounting already makes crash 1 more cautious than the scored reached − crash (a crash at ~2 s weighs 0.74, a success at ~8 s 0.45), so half the cost may score better.

**Time cost 0.004 per decision:** a whole timed-out episode costs 1 goal, the largest level that keeps waiting cheaper than the smallest crash cost tested (0.5). Aimed at the FRONT give-up.

Step 8c2 added a time cost, because 8c measured that every crash cost makes runs give up on FRONT (timeouts of 10% or more).

## Approach

Three rounds of 2M transitions on the Windows PC, each starting from the previous round's trained policy (same seed); round 1 from fresh weights, success only.

| Run | Arm | Task | Reward | Transitions | Starts from | Trained on |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `N1` | full task | +1 reach, crash 0 | 2M | fresh weights | PC |
| 2 | `N2_c05w0t4` | full task | +1 reach, −0.5 crash, −0.004/decision | 2M | `N1`'s trained policy (same seed) | PC |
| 3 | `N3_c05w0t4` | full task | +1 reach, −0.5 crash, −0.004/decision | 2M | `N2_c05w0t4`'s trained policy (same seed) | PC |

## Reward function

Of the last run, `N3_c05w0t4` (per decision, in goal units after the reward scale):

- +1 when ego reaches the slot (the episode ends)
- −0.5 on a crash (the episode ends)
- −0.004 per decision (0.1 s); a whole 25 s episode costs 1
- 0 on a timeout (25 s) or a lost anchor

Written out: `r = 0.02 × (50·[reached] − 25·[crash] − 0.2 per decision)`, where [reached] and [crash] are 1 on the decision the episode ends that way and 0 otherwise.

Earlier runs of the pipeline used:

- `N1`: +1 reach, crash 0 (`r = 0.02 × (50·[reached])`)

## Analysis

- On the screening seeds (7, 10, 13) it ranks 23 of the 26 64-64 pipelines (score +0.490; the best is O_c1w01, +0.697).
- Against success only, same order (`N3_c0w0`): -0.044 (95% interval -0.679 to +0.592, p 0.80, ahead on 1 of 3 seeds; crash rate -2.8 points).
- Against P6's reward, same order (`N3_c1w01`): -0.151 (95% interval -0.647 to +0.346, p 0.32, ahead on 1 of 3 seeds; crash rate +12.1 points).
- Against order O, same reward (`O3_c05w0t4`): -0.139 (95% interval -0.548 to +0.269, p 0.28, ahead on 1 of 3 seeds; crash rate +2.1 points).

Across 8c2: a time cost moves the give-up but does not remove the trade. Where t4 ends it (crash 0.5), part of the timeouts become reaches and part crashes. No time-cost cell is beyond noise of its no-time-cost cell.
