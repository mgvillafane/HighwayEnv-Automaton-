# O_c05w0: original order, c05w0

*Step 8c, trained 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-20. Seeds: 7, 10, 13. [All attempts](README.md) · [Experiment notes](../EXPERIMENT_NOTES.md)*

**Status:** Screened in Step 8c on 3 seeds; not a finalist.

## Idea

**Order O, the original order.** P4 → P5 → P6 at 2M per round: round 1 practice with easy starts, success only (P4); round 2 the same practice with the reward under test (P5); round 3 the full task (P6).

**Crash cost 0.5 × goal.** Discounting already makes crash 1 more cautious than the scored reached − crash (a crash at ~2 s weighs 0.74, a success at ~8 s 0.45), so half the cost may score better.

Step 8c was the reward grid on orders N and O: crash 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 × progress 0 / 0.1, 3 seeds. The rule picks the simplest reward that the best one does not beat beyond noise.

## Approach

Three rounds of 2M transitions on the Windows PC, each starting from the previous round's trained policy (same seed); round 1 from fresh weights, success only.

| Run | Arm | Task | Reward | Transitions | Starts from | Trained on |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `E1` | practice with easy starts | +1 reach, crash 0 | 2M | fresh weights | PC |
| 2 | `O2_c05w0` | practice with easy starts | +1 reach, −0.5 crash | 2M | `E1`'s trained policy (same seed) | PC |
| 3 | `O3_c05w0` | full task | +1 reach, −0.5 crash | 2M | `O2_c05w0`'s trained policy (same seed) | PC |

## Reward function

Of the last run, `O3_c05w0` (per decision, in goal units after the reward scale):

- +1 when ego reaches the slot (the episode ends)
- −0.5 on a crash (the episode ends)
- 0 on a timeout (25 s) or a lost anchor

Written out: `r = 0.02 × (50·[reached] − 25·[crash])`, where [reached] and [crash] are 1 on the decision the episode ends that way and 0 otherwise.

Earlier runs of the pipeline used:

- `E1`: +1 reach, crash 0 (`r = 0.02 × (50·[reached])`)

## Analysis

- On the screening seeds (7, 10, 13) it ranks 14 of the 26 64-64 pipelines (score +0.578; the best is O_c1w01, +0.697).
- Against success only, same order (`O3_c0w0`): -0.103 (95% interval -0.357 to +0.152, p 0.22, ahead on 0 of 3 seeds; crash rate -2.6 points).
- Against P6's reward, same order (`O3_c1w01`): -0.119 (95% interval -0.510 to +0.271, p 0.32, ahead on 1 of 3 seeds; crash rate +5.2 points).
- Against order N, same reward (`N3_c05w0`): +0.043 (95% interval -0.391 to +0.477, p 0.71, ahead on 1 of 3 seeds; crash rate +1.2 points).

Across 8c: any crash cost brings the FRONT give-up, and success only never does. The score swaps crashes for timeouts almost one for one. Nothing beat success only by the rule with 3 seeds; P6's reward scored highest in both orders.
